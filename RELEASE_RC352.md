# RC352: cultural recordings and wider rehearsal alternatives

Adds the owner's Woro, Highlife and Ariaria material to Basslines, Runs, My fills and Rehearsal. Rehearsal now offers bassline, run and fill alternatives across the visible repertoire, including Worship, R&B, Gospel, Soul, Reggae, Jazz, Blues and the existing cultural styles. All previous rehearsal genre choices remain. Bass Lady, Bassmatics and World Methods can also be chosen as sources.

The four practice buttons now have separate colours: blue Basslines, purple Runs, amber My fills and rose Rehearse song. Alternative names appear before their source labels so they remain readable on a phone.

## Recordings

Reviewed 96 active passages across the 24 supplied recordings. Retained 45 returning loops from 20 recordings: 17 Ariaria, 16 Highlife, nine Woro and three Makossa. The Tobi Jeff Richards medley is Ariaria, as confirmed by the owner. Display names omit the downloader prefix. Each recording has original audio, synchronised estimated bass/drum/other channels and a brief variation label. Twenty estimated source drum loops support the corresponding derived studies.

Four sources had no qualifying returning phrase in the reviewed passages: African ARIARIA Bass Secret, Intense Ariaria Praise and Worship, PRAISE FROM WITHIN and Hot Naija Woro Praise Medley. The raw files have not been added as unfinished exercises. Notes, tempo and separation remain estimates. Full recordings were scanned for active regions; this is not a verified transcription of all 334.6 minutes.

Adds 221 source-derived run/fill studies while preserving all 208 RC351 studies, their IDs and musical data. All 429 cultural studies support the 42 directed changes between different degrees 1–7 in all 12 keys. Existing deletions and permanent removals remain effective.

## Rehearsal

Alternatives follow the song's saved harmony, including actual pitch-class and slash-bass changes. Captured approaches are adapted to the destination; they do not replace saved chord analysis. Suitable studies can supply practice accompaniment motifs or transitions where a genre lacks a separate bassline or run. Adaptations are labelled. Empty genre repertoires retain Roots rather than reviving deleted recordings.

Placement supports every bar, chord changes or off. Extremely short, dense or uncertain transitions are skipped; partial final bars are bounded safely. Changing choices keeps the current band loop running and applies the replacement guide at a loop boundary. Recorded exercises retain bass 100%, drums 50%, other instruments 50%; Rehearsal retains guide 100% and music 50%.

## Validation

- New audio: all 45 six-channel mixes and 20 drum loops checked for finite samples, channel layout, source reconstruction, peaks, duration and continuous joins.
- Existing and new cultural changes: 216,216 transposed combinations checked, plus preservation of the earlier 8,400 legacy transitions.
- Rehearsal: all three alternative types available in 20 populated genres; 118 choices fitted to real chord boundaries; source and pattern deletion checked after reload.
- Mobile browser: four distinct button colours, 588 cultural degree-control checks, actual recorded 100/50/50 playback, 17 genre/source selectors and continuous rehearsal playback during changes.
- Existing catalogue, FL drum assignments, permanent deletion, mixer defaults and rehearsal regressions passed.

Offline audio preparation uses a verified local model with OpenCV's built-in CPU DNN backend. No Python ONNX Runtime or telemetry client is used. Model weights and private checkpoints stay outside the repository.
