# RC356: alternatives inside the original phrase timeframe

Adds timestamped occurrence choices to the existing change selector. “Changes” retains grouped movements; “Phrases” can select an individual occurrence such as “5→6 · 1:02–1:05”. Source details repeat the selected range. No additional display rows are added.

For a timed phrase, run and fill attacks are fitted to that phrase's saved beat positions. The alternative starts on the source bass at the exact phrase start and lands on the destination at the original timestamp. Focused bassline alternatives stop replacing the following chord's whole section. Other occurrences and surrounding notes remain unchanged, apart from necessary boundary clipping of a note crossing into the replacement.

Whole-progression and grouped movement choices remain. The existing colours, note timestamps, genre/source choices, harmony rules, mixer defaults and audio-clock playback remain.

Validation uses two occurrences of 5→6 in one fixture, at 0:10–0:13 and 1:02–1:05. Across twenty populated genres and all three alternative types, the selected phrase starts on 5 at 62 seconds and lands on 6 at 65 seconds, with the other occurrence and notes outside the selected interval preserved. Existing key/chord and phone playback checks cover the updated controls, exact endpoints and continuous changes.

These are source adaptations using saved song timing and harmony; source recordings do not establish verified original chord progressions. Publishing follows the owner's standing authorisation.
