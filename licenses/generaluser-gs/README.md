The drum performances in audio/drums-rc345 are original arrangements rendered with GeneralUser GS by S. Christian Collins. Source: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS

The fingered bass in audio/bass-rc346 uses GeneralUser GS v2.0.3, bank 0, program 33 (zero-based). Thirteen pitches from B0 to B3 were rendered at velocities 64 and 104, with effects disabled. Playback blends the two layers and preserves the existing note timings and bass register. Grand piano and recorded performances keep their established sounds.
