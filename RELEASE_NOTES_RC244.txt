RC244: match lyrics playback to the selected recording; remove To import & analyse

The complete worklist/discovery feature, its card and its analysis adapter are removed.
Song chords and Lyrics now share a two-column row of equal-height cards.
The other tool rows, ordinary import, batch analysis and Back navigation are retained.

Lyrics search now previews the selected song separately from loaded lyrics/chords.
Play checks the title and recording duration, plus artist metadata where available.
It uses matching loaded audio or a single matching saved project. Ambiguous or missing
recordings require the user to load the audio. Mismatched titles/durations are blocked.
Searching works without audio already loaded. LRCLIB supplies no audio stream.
Choosing lyrics does not overwrite the loaded song's words. Replacement is confirmed
only after matching audio is loaded. Closing/changing the selection cancels pending playback.
No new analysis job is submitted by lyric search or playback.

Validation: RC244 playback regression tests, LRCLIB parsing/integration, lyric controls,
tool layout, nested Back navigation, inline script syntax and whitespace checks passed.
Two historical tests also fail on the unchanged baseline: lyrics_rc169 expects an old
audio graph; save_import_rc164 omits the newer importAudioCheckingLibrary test stub.
Chromium launch is blocked by the environment's socket policy; no visual/audio browser
verification or paid RunPod job was performed.
