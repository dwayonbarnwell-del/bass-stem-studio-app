# RC359: clearer bass roles and a compact transport

The rehearsal transport places fill/run placement, original bass mute, pause/play and guide bass mute in one row. Short labels and speaker/playback icons retain full accessible names and tooltips. The view row has a controls button for explicitly revealing setup and volume sliders. Playback still opens the compact view immediately.

Pause retains the compact view, timestamp, note page, fret view and repeat selections. Resume starts the backing and guide at the paused source position on the same audio clock. Showing controls does not pause or restart playback.

Note View distinguishes Main (the current chord's actual bass pitch, including inversions), Chord, Fill/run and Passing notes. Main notes have a gold rounded-square shape; generated fills/runs are purple with F/R badges, and inferred passing notes have a dashed outline. A root inside a generated fill/run retains its F/R badge. Uncertain harmony remains unclassified. A note at a chord boundary is no longer marked as a landing merely because of its timestamp.

Timestamp headers show the current chord's degree and pitch, for example 4 F. Amber previews show the next directed change only while approaching it; the landing then shows the actual current chord. Same-root quality changes no longer display false 4→4 movements. Harmony and note/audio timing remain unchanged, and the fitter retains all its original harmonic events.

Checks cover note roles, inversions, uncertain harmony, false boundary landings, retained pause position and same-clock resume, repeated selections, continuous style/mix changes and the existing key/mode and whole-song fitting cases. Phone layout checks include dense notes and both full-height fretboards.

The duplicate Sources group is removed from the Rehearsal genre menu. Every genre retains the same recordings and source metadata. All pattern/recording captions across Basslines, Runs, My fills and Rehearsal fit 12 characters, omit repeated genres, preserve source prefixes and distinct variation codes, and avoid cutting a phrase halfway through a word. For example, World Methods 12 · Makossa becomes World 12 inside Makossa. Full names and provenance remain in details/tooltips. Progressions remain visible in the harmony display and option details. Genre, band and practice action labels are also shortened. Stable IDs, musical patterns, deletions and audio assignments are preserved.
