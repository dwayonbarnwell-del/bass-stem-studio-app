# RC360: balanced rehearsal controls and compact timing cards

The four playback controls use equal-width, equal-height cards. Original and guide bass controls place the speaker icon below the short label while retaining full accessible names and mute states.

In Note View, the view selector uses about half the row and the settings/back/next controls share the other half. Additional follow/clear actions remain usable when required; very narrow screens use an extra row rather than tiny controls or horizontal overflow. Fret and Phrase views keep the available width when timeline paging is not needed.

Start, End and BPM are flat 44px cards with the label and editable value inside the same border. Focus feedback, exact values and editing handlers remain.

This update changes presentation only. All musical fitting, timing, source names, defaults, pause/resume, repeat selections and full-height fret views remain in place. Validation uses the existing playback controller checks and real phone layouts, including timing edits and repeat-navigation states.

Phone checks: at 390px, the Note View selector is about 159px and its three buttons are 53px each. Playback cards are equally 79px by 44px. At 320px the playback cards remain equal at about 62px by 44px, and all five repeat tools fit without horizontal overflow. A dense page of 37 notes fits without scrolling, and both fret views retain 550px height. The timing cards measure 44px with no nested input border, display 7:55.54 in full, and retain editable Start/End/BPM values. Short placement captions and inset chevrons keep dropdown text readable.
