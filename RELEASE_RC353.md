# RC353: clear rehearsal source names

Different rehearsal choices were collapsing to the same shortened names, such as “Pattern”, while repeating “Practice (adapted)” made the phone menu too long.

Choices now use a short source, reference and genre, for example “Bass Lady 01 · Makossa”, “Bassmatics 01 · Seben” or “Practice 03 · Gospel”. The selected source and genre appear beneath the menu, with “Adapted” shown once. Bassline alternatives is shortened to Basslines.

Names use existing source IDs, source families and genre metadata. Generated material remains labelled Practice. Known Bass Lady and Bassmatics recording numbers are retained, and deleting a choice does not renumber its siblings. Unknown genres remain unconfirmed. Original titles remain available in the option's title.

No study IDs, musical patterns, source recordings, genre choices, mixer defaults, deletions or playback behaviour change.

Validation: compared every available genre/source bank with RC352 for identical IDs, order, rhythms and fitted notes; checked short unique labels and actual teacher metadata; exercised the phone controls, source readout and uninterrupted playback. The standing publishing authorisation applies.
