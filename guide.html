Bass Stem Studio

Practice tools for bass players and other musicians. Use your own songs and keep the parts you have already analysed.

Open Bass Stem Studio

Get started

  1. Open the app and choose an audio file you own or are allowed to use.
  2. Select Analyse to review bass notes, chords and song sections. The results are estimates and may need correction.
  3. Use the playback and practice tools to focus on short parts or repeat a section.
  4. Save your project in Library so you can open it again without repeating a completed analysis.

See a simple song chart

Select More tools → Song chords, search for a song and open its numbered Intro, Verse, Chorus and other recorded parts. Select Edit progression to make changes.

You can use Add charts from saved library to build simple draft charts from compatible saved .bassstudio or JSON analyses. Check these drafts against the recording.

Remove several songs

In Song chords, use Select songs and then Select all matching or choose individual songs. Remove selected puts them in a recoverable list. Under Show → Removed songs you can restore them, or permanently delete your own saved songs after confirmation.

Keep backups safe

In Song chords, choose Share backup or Export backup. Save a copy outside your browser. Local files and progressions are not automatically synced to other devices.

Share with another musician

Use the Share app link button above. The app is accessible by its public web address. Other users need their own audio and any required analysis access. Some online analysis may incur charges.
