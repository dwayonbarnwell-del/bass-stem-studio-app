# RC294

Supplied drum loops, continuous practice playback and a compact Ear training view.

- Prepared 45 loops from the owner's two drum archives. Kept labelled tempos; the remaining tempos come from eight-bar duration or are marked as estimates. Excluded the snare demonstration from continuous beats.
- Grand piano is the automatic keyboard sound. Worship uses the supplied 80 BPM R&B loop, with other soul/funk recordings available and adjustable tempo.
- Bass, piano and drums share an integer-sample beat. Percussion stretching anchors attacks to their original musical positions.
- Play repeats automatically. Sound, style, tempo, key, pattern, practice section and Next changes prepare the replacement while the current loop continues, then switch at a bar boundary. Stop cancels pending changes. Muting and volume changes preserve the running transport.
- Shortened study names, retained progression numbers and stable IDs, added blue active chord indicators, and removed the requested recordings and descriptive paragraphs. Compact controls leave more room for notes.
- Original recordings retain their own accompaniment; unverified beat grids do not receive added drums. Short transcribed excerpts use native audio-buffer looping; long recordings retain streamed playback.

Validation: 107,268 existing arrangement/extraction checks; 3,696 accompaniment/harmony checks; all 45 audio files decoded in Chrome with native lengths verified; real audio checks at 40, 80, 137 and 220 BPM; clipping and repeated-buffer drift checks; live control/Next/section changes, rapid changes and Stop during loading; 390px phone layout inspected. Synthetic attack timing checks across 40–220 BPM stayed within 3.3 ms. This is a technical timing check, not a claim that every source performance is quantised.
