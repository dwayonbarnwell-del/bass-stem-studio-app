Bass Stem Studio RC160: narrower time columns

- Halved timeline column widths from RC159 in portrait and landscape.
- Shows twice as many time columns at once.
- Reflows note and chord labels to keep the chosen font size readable.
- Preserves every note/chord and automatically follows the active events.
- Existing device rotation, settings location, repeat controls and note editing remain.

Checks
- Compared RC159 and RC160 with the same fixture at 360, 390, 740 and 844px.
  Every measured timeline column was exactly 50% narrower (within 0.1px).
- Note labels fit at 14px without reducing the selected font size.
- Existing repeat, inline edit, import isolation, active-note/chord following,
  rotation during playback and six-string Bass View checks passed.
- No backend deployment or paid analysis tests were needed.
