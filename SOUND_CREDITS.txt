Salamander Grand Piano v3 (RC257)
Author: Alexander Holm. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
Original recording: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Source commit: 3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Full licence and upstream description: ai-source/salamander/LICENSE.txt and SOURCE_README.md.
Adaptation: layers 4/8/12 selected; original minor-third anchors retained; source retuning corrections baked in; upstream start offsets shortened with a 3 ms lead; tails limited to 5–8 seconds with a 250 ms fade; encoded to 44.1 kHz stereo MP3. Relative sample levels preserved.
Derived from the SFZ mapping by kinwie, which credits Jeff Learman for the fast-response offsets. Separate pedal, hammer and sympathetic-resonance recordings omitted for this compact browser version.

Virtuosity Drums (RC292)
Versilian Studios LLC and Karoryfer Samples; performed by Austin McMahon.
https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/
CC0 1.0 Universal. Full licence, provenance and edits: audio/drums-rc292/.
Three microphone/articulation selections from the same acoustic kit: Warm Vintage, Tight Studio and Open Jazz. Original practice grooves were written for Bass Stem Studio.

RC293 genre keyboard accompaniment
Wurlitzer EP200 recordings by Greg Sullivan; SFZ mapping/tuning by kinwie. CC BY 3.0.
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
Commit 8c3e581acda3594b553948ff0222d4f84a698376.
Drawbar Organ Emulation by Roberto for FreePats, sampled from setBfree. CC0.
https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Organ/electric-organ.html
This is a sampled organ emulation, not a physical Hammond recording.
Adaptations: selected roots/velocities, source tuning and gain, mono PCM16 at 32 kHz, preserved sustain loops. Full source metadata, attribution and licences: audio/keys-rc293/.

Groove MIDI Dataset drum performances (CC BY 4.0)
Two-bar excerpts retain the performers’ timing and dynamics and use the Virtuosity Drums sounds credited above. Source performances, performers, release details, attribution and modifications are listed in licenses/groove-midi/sources.txt.

RC346 fingered practice bass: GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins. Rendered note performances at two velocities, with original note timing, a warm bass filter and neighbouring-note pitch shifting. Source: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS .
