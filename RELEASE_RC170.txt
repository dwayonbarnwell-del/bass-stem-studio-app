Bass Stem Studio RC170

Ideas
Import and analyse a song, move to the desired part, then choose Ideas. Select Jazz, Gospel, Reggae, Funk, Soul / R&B or Pop. Current section starts at the beginning of the section containing the playhead. Each request uses up to 8 reliable chord changes within 30 seconds. Adjacent matching chords are merged.

Light suggestions add chord colour for Jazz, Gospel and Soul. Bold jazz can substitute a resolving dominant seventh with its tritone substitute. Slash-bass chords are preserved. Bass sketches use genre-specific note patterns; passing notes approach the next suggested bass root by semitone. Notes stay within E1–G3. No melody compatibility guarantee is made.

Chord guide previews the analysed harmony with root/slash bass. Hear idea previews the proposed harmony and bass sketch. Both use synthesised tones, not the original recording or a generated full arrangement. Stop and Close release preview audio. Save ideas downloads a CSV with times, original chords, proposed chords, bass notes and style. It does not edit the song, notes, original recording or saved analysis. Suggestions combine existing AI-detected chords with deterministic music-theory rules; no new generative AI service or new model training was added.
