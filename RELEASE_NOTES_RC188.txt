Bass Stem Studio RC188

Timeline intervals now offer every whole second from 1 to 10. Older saved intervals above 10 open at 10 seconds; song sections and their times are retained.

Restored the Text size card by removing obsolete Compare and Setlist shortcut code that interrupted startup. Presets and custom sizes keep the existing display settings.

The BPM card now opens a playback tempo editor. BPM and Speed use the same pitch-preserving playback rate for the main, bass and backing tracks. Original tempo resets Speed to 100%. Custom speeds are retained in saved projects; the analysed source tempo and beat positions are unchanged. Playback speed is bounded to 25–200%; the editor validates the corresponding BPM range. The card is unavailable until a song tempo is known.

Removed the main Undo edit and Reanalyse selection cards and the Edit undone message. Note editing and its inline Undo remain available. Other analysis notices remain plain status text.

Validation: script parsing, shortcut element references, interval choices, BPM/Speed calculations, all track rates, custom saved-speed restoration, invalid input bounds, saved sections, ear training and automatic audio level checks. Physical-device listening is not included.
