# RC289: ranked runs and fills

Added 110 explicitly composed, source-informed practice phrases: 55 Runs and 55 My fills. Each category has 11 contemporary/gospel, 11 jazz, 11 blues, 11 pentatonic and 11 funk/fusion phrases. Each phrase contains 3–12 notes, excluding the opening root and final landing. Existing entries and deletion keys are preserved.

Both categories sort Most amazing → Excellent → Good, with independent style, rating and collection filters. Filters persist on the device. Editorial ratings reflect melodic interest, groove, contrast and resolution; they are subjective and separate from difficulty. Original-study labelling and technique-source links appear beside the selected phrase. The source pages inform general musical techniques; these are not transcriptions or endorsements by the named musicians.

Public sources reviewed on 17 November 2026:

- [Daric Bennett: Gospel Fill Lesson](https://daricbennett.com/free-bass-lessons/gospel-fill-lesson/): diminished tension and resolution. Only public lesson information was used.
- [Damian Erskine: Ask Damian](https://www.notreble.com/buzz/2009/06/24/ask-damian-erskine/): chord-tone inversions and voice leading.
- [Rich Brown: pentatonic fills](https://www.notreble.com/buzz/2021/10/20/the-brownstone-play-great-fills-and-solos-using-pentatonics/): melodic pentatonic applications.
- [Mark Smith: blues approaches](https://www.talkingbass.net/big-badass-blues-rock-bass-lesson/): short chromatic approaches, blues colour and leaving space.
- [Mark Smith: Pentatonic Funk Chops](https://www.talkingbass.net/pentatonic-funk-chops/): pentatonic vocabulary, rhythmic phrasing and fretboard movement.
- [Nick Campbell: Pentatonic Voice Leading](https://scottsbasslessons.com/courses/pentatonic-voice-leading-with-nick-campbell): public course overview only; no paid lesson content was accessed or copied.
