Six new runs and 31 new fills were explicitly composed. The existing Highlife ribbon and Gospel descending thirds were identical; they now share one entry, with both names retained in its heading. All previous musical content is retained. Stable legacy IDs preserve deletions even after index consolidation, including deletions under the duplicate's former name.

Selection prioritises rhythmic character, melodic contour, contrast, register changes and a strong downbeat resolution. Phrases remain original studies, not artist transcriptions, endorsements or audience-rated hits. Note playback demonstrates the pitches and rhythm; performers supply expressive articulation. Counts are equal between Runs and My fills, not forced equal across styles, which would require discarding existing material.
