# RC292: lower bass and timed acoustic drums

Ear-training bass previously reached MIDI 78 (F♯5) in the practice catalogue. Practice playback now fits B0–B3, preferring B2 or below whenever an octave shift preserves the phrase. In 118,884 tested arrangements, 91,638 fit entirely at B2 or below. Only 61 unusually wide/key-dependent arrangements require individual octave changes; these show “Octaves adjusted”. Interval questions preserve their exact semitone distances. Original recordings keep their pitch.

The existing sampled fingered bass now uses a gentle low shelf and treble roll-off, with velocity-sensitive levels. Notes ear training opens with Bass by default and remembers an explicit Piano selection. Piano chord backing is off initially, with a separate control.

Three drum sounds (Warm Vintage, Tight Studio, Open Jazz) use 36 edited CC0 Virtuosity Drums recordings, about 3 MB in total. These are microphone/articulation selections from the same acoustic kit, not three separately recorded kits. Eight original 4/4 grooves, a volume control and on/off control are included. Reference MP3s were not redistributed. Provenance, transformations and original licence are under `audio/drums-rc292/`.

Timed studies render bass, chords and drums once, then play native AudioBufferSource loops on one audio-clock boundary. Repeat has no recursive timeout restart or additional loading gap. Sound toggles change gain without restarting the loop. UI animation follows that audio clock. Loaded drum samples are cached locally. Playback stops when the page is hidden, as before.

Recordings do not contain verified beat grids, so additional drums are unavailable there. Long recorded lessons keep direct note scheduling to avoid allocating twenty-minute PCM buffers on a phone. Their note-repeat scheduler uses absolute boundaries and stops rather than playing a missed boundary late. Original recording playback and pitch-preserving speed controls remain unchanged.

## Validation

- `node tests/practice_audio_rc292.cjs`: 118,884 arrangements in range; pitch classes and note timing retained; 3,000 bass questions checked; 3,000 piano questions unchanged; eight deterministic grooves; shared-clock transport and repeat toggles passed.
- Existing catalogue and ear-level checks passed (22,428 arrangements and 7,000 advanced questions).
- Real Web Audio browser checks passed for all three sounds: sample decoding, audible unclipped mixes, six identical audio-loop cycles within one sample, live repeat, volume/mute changes without restarting, replay cache, stop/section changes, saved drum choices and long recorded-lesson note playback.
- Phone preview at 390 × 844: controls fit without horizontal overflow; three transport buttons above two sound toggles; full-width drum selectors.
- JavaScript syntax and `git diff --check` passed.

Actual Android hardware, Bluetooth output and subjective speaker tone were not directly tested. A phone speaker may not reproduce the fundamental of very low bass notes.
