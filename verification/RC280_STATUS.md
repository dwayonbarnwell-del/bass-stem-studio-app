# RC280: another 1,000 songs with progressions

Date: 16 November 2026. Baseline: RC279, main ba04cfcbc27a0a22f00ef550653815e3068a1083.

Adds exactly 1,000 songs and 6,343 numbered section progressions from public Psalmnote community charts. The catalogue now contains 3,944 entries, of which 3,809 have progressions. All 2,944 previous song records and IDs remain unchanged. No lyrics or audio are imported.

Duplicate checks cover all existing titles and aliases, independent of artist, with punctuation, accents, recording suffixes, featured artists, numbering, articles and common contractions normalised. Close title variations and medleys are excluded. Every addition contains at least two supported sections and at least one verse, chorus, refrain or bridge. The source manifest records each source URL, key, source hash and omitted sections.

These are community harmonic references, not independently verified transcriptions of recordings. Of the additions, 580 carry the source's verification badge. 754 charts have fully supported source sections; 246 retain supported sections and explicitly disclose that unclear sections or key changes were omitted. Repeated chords and exact repeated cycles are condensed. Sections over 20 changes are labelled opening progressions. Unbadged charts with unsupported sections are excluded.

The existing New additions filter now selects RC280's 1,000 songs. Song Chords layout, storage, user edits, favourites, removals and earlier additions are preserved. The app version and service-worker cache advance to RC280.

Validation: the dedicated catalogue suite passes counts, all existing records, unique titles and IDs, valid numeric chords, source links, New additions, sourced filters, saved edits, favourites and persistent removals. All inline scripts and changed JavaScript parse. Existing editing and playback timing tests also pass. Local graphical preview was inaccessible from the cloud browser; verify the deployed catalogue before reporting completion.
