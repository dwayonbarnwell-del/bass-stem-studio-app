# Acoustic drums for Bass Stem Studio

These are selected and edited recordings from **Virtuosity Drums**, by Versilian Studios LLC and Karoryfer Samples, performed by Austin McMahon at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston, MA.

Source: https://versilian-studios.com/virtuosity-drums/
Repository: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The original licence is included in LICENSE-CC0.txt.

Three microphone/articulation selections provide different sounds from the same recorded acoustic kit:

- **Warm Vintage:** vintage microphone and hand-muted snare.
- **Tight Studio:** close kick/snare microphones and mid-position cymbals.
- **Open Jazz:** mid-position kick/snare and overhead cymbals.

Each hit has two separate recorded strikes. Snare alternates are adjacent recorded velocity layers; other hits use the source round-robin recordings. A separate soft-snare pair supplies ghost notes. The player sets velocity and instrument balance.

Preparation: mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz; source pre-roll trimmed; very short attack ramp; short faded tails; peaks normalised to 0.79. See manifest.json for every source URL, immutable source commit, trim amount and checksums.

The user's two reference MP3 files are not included or sampled.
