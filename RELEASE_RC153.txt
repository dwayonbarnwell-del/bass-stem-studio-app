How to read the chord row
With 2s selected, a cell covers two seconds. A vertical list represents the
app's estimated sequence of chord changes during that interval. It is not
a list of alternatives to choose from. A purple ? means the individual
estimate is uncertain. Rapid apparent changes may include detection errors;
this display update does not establish that all chord detections are correct.
