Worship and gospel chord changes

  1. KyleTeachesBass · The three-note hinge

    Play the connection into 4, then move the same idea to land on 5.

  2. ResponsiBass · Worship moves for 1–4–5–4–5–1

    Keep the pulse on 1 and listen for the pickup into 4.

  3. ResponsiBass · Worship moves for 1–3–4–3–6–2–5

    Isolate 6→2 before playing the whole progression.

  4. Jermaine Morgan · Filling the gaps in gospel

    Sing the vocal line. Play the fill where the singer takes a breath.

  5. Gospel fills over 1–7–6–5–4–3–2

    Follow the descending roots and listen for where the player changes register.

  6. Gospel passing notes into 1, 4 and 5

    Choose one destination and practise two different approaches to it.

  7. Worship passing notes into 4, 1, 5, 6 and 2

    Sing the destination note before you play the pickup.

  8. Worship passing notes into 1, 6, 2, 4 and 5

    Move one short answer to a different chord without moving the downbeat.

  9. Worship bass fills, step by step

    Separate the quick notes from the held landing. Learn the rhythm first.

  10. Scott’s Bass Lessons and Andrew Gouché · Shout bass

    Keep the repeated pulse steady as the upper notes move.

Phrasing, soul and R&B

  1. TalkingBass · Creating bass fills step by step

    Take one bar of groove and choose the last note you can leave out before the fill.

  2. Ran Levi · Building basslines from one lick

  3. Daric Bennett · Bass riff concepts

    Play an idea in two registers and listen for which one leaves space.

  4. Why your bass fills fall flat

    Clap the fill against a steady pulse before choosing the notes.
