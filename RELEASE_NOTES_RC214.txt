Bass Stem Studio RC214: Show usable chords only and repair Song Chords access

- The currently deployed RC213 release had a Song chords button under More tools, but its shortcut inside Library failed to appear: Library is built on demand after RC213 had already tried to add the button. RC214 now installs the button each time Library is opened. Existing audio Library and its controls remain unchanged.
- The Song Chords search shows only entries with at least one saved chord progression. Blank song titles are retained safely in the internal catalogue for later additions, but hidden from all user-facing searches, filters and counts. No fake progressions added.
- Add or retain 11 published Nigerian music blog chord references, including seven additional named songs (Imela, Olowogbogboro, I Have Come to Worship, Eze, Chinedum, Ekwueme, Most High), four previously blank titles (Only You Jesus, Eben's Victory, We go dey hail, Steve Crown's You Are Great), plus shorter alternative references for three existing personal chord sheets. Earlier user-written notes are preserved, marked unverified and editable.
- Visible library contains 364 entries with progressions. An additional 501 titles without chorded sections are not displayed. 35 Nigerian collection entries and 10 CCLI Top 100 entries currently have progressions; neither chart is falsely advertised as having 100 usable arrangements.
- Credited blog charts are explicitly marked as blog-derived, not independently checked for the matching recording. User corrections override default data without overwriting previous saves. Reference links are optional: the short progressions display offline in the app itself.
- Song Chords available under More tools and within Library (new shortcut). Offline assets are in the service worker cache RC214; initial online load is required for the browser to cache them. Use Export backup to protect local edits. No separate download or RunPod job is necessary.
- Tests: Node syntax checks of both JS files; Playwright Chromium at 390, 800, 1280 pixels: Library shortcut, hiding unchorded songs, Nigerian blog chart examples, search, editing, saved result after reopening, no JavaScript errors. No paid RunPod inference performed.
- Sources include michaelphils.com, mynaijapianist.wordpress.com, naijamediamp3.blogspot.com, yallemedia.com, and worshipchords.net. Only compact musical progressions are stored, not bulk lyrics or full third-party arrangements.
