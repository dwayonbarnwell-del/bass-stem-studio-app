# RC351

Runs and My fills now split African Grooves into individual cultural styles. Makossa, Seben, Soukous and Zouk have source-derived exercises; Highlife, Afrobeat, Sungura, Mbalax, Mbaqanga and African praise appear where material is available. Empty styles are omitted from each menu. The four existing non-cultural groups remain available.

Added 99 runs and 109 fills from the supplied Bass Lady, Bassmatics and World Methods passages and the app's other cultural basslines. Every exercise supports all 42 directed changes between different degrees 1–7, in every key. This includes 4→7, 1→7, 6→7 and reverse changes. The former African Grooves preference migrates to Makossa.

Exercises retain a contiguous source passage's attack order and relative timing. Matching source intervals sort first. For other changes, the captured final approach is transposed into the selected landing while retaining the internal intervals within that approach. Source names and adaptations are labelled. These are practice adaptations of automatically estimated notes, not claims about the recording's verified original chord progression.

The source repertoire is checked when the app loads, so newly added or individually restored cultural basslines can contribute matching exercises after refresh. Source and pattern deletions remain effective, including permanent Empty deleted decisions. A deleted source must be restored before its deleted derivative can be restored. Existing bassline menus are not reinstated by the new Runs/My fills groups.

Genre routing uses stable source-study IDs, preserving the existing uploaded drum assignments. The requested bass 100%, drums 50% and keys/other 50% defaults remain in place, with saved manual edits retained. Changing chords, style or pattern and pressing Next continue on the audio clock.

Validation:

- All 104,832 exercise/change/key combinations passed root, landing, range, timing and register checks.
- All 8,400 existing run/fill transitions retain their previous musical signatures and IDs.
- Browser checks exercised all 42 changes in Makossa, Seben, Soukous and Zouk for both Runs and My fills, 336 real control combinations.
- Real audio checks passed the 100/50/50 mix, continuous chord changes, Next and style changes.
- Source labels, short unique names, source dependencies, individual restoration and persistent permanent deletion passed.
- The phone layout was checked at 390 × 850.
