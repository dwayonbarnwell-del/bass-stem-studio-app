RC242: piano view and melody reuse

- Full current chord and melody note remain readable below the timeline.
- Keyboard range changes less often; transposition keeps its range aligned.
- Short notes keep exact timing, with full names in the readout and editor.
- Practice controls fit within the screen, including small phones and landscape.
- Melody analysis and corrections save automatically with the local song project.
- Audio-content identity reuses completed melody jobs across renamed/reopened files.
- Newer local melody corrections are recovered when an older JSON is reopened.
- Completed analysis shows Melody ready. No paid work is triggered by playback or exports.
- Bundled sampled grand piano and fingered electric bass replace the oscillator sound.
- WAV exports use 44.1 kHz; MP3 uses 192 kbps. No external sound service required.

Validation: simulated whole-song jobs, interrupted jobs, cached reopening, browser playback and editing, local project saving, phone/landscape layouts, export formats and sample audio checks. No paid RunPod jobs or reanalysis of the user's song were performed.
